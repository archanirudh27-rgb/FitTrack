FitTrack

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

FitTrack is a personal fitness application for workout tracking and optional wearable health insights. Your data is used only to provide your FitTrack experience.

Health data

If you connect Health Connect, FitTrack requests read-only access to health categories you choose, such as steps, distance, calories, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep, oxygen saturation, respiratory rate and exercise sessions. FitTrack does not use your phone GPS to track walks, runs or cycling in the background.

How data is stored

FitTrack stores compact health summaries and imported activity sessions in the FitTrack database associated with your signed-in account. Raw continuous sensor streams are not intentionally uploaded. Your workout and health records are separated by user account.

Sharing

FitTrack does not sell health data or personal information to advertisers. Data is processed only by services required to operate FitTrack, including its database/authentication provider.

Your control

Health permissions can be revoked in Health Connect at any time. FitTrack also provides a Reset fitness data control that removes your stored fitness records while preserving your account unless you choose otherwise.

Permissions

FitTrack asks only for health permissions needed for the dashboard features. A wearable or its companion app must first make a metric available to Health Connect before FitTrack can read it.

Contact

For questions about FitTrack privacy, contact the person who shared the FitTrack app with you.
